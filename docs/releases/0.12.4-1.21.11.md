Latest release as of 4 Nov. Both jars are attached: `+1.21.11` for Minecraft 1.21.11 and `+26.3` for Minecraft 26.3. Put the one for your version in your `mods` folder with Fabric API and Sodium.

## New since 0.12.3

**Modrinth browser: icons and a Tiles view**
- Every result shows the project's real icon from Modrinth instead of a coloured letter, in the tiles, the list and the details panel. Icons load in the background and are kept on disk, so the next visit is instant.
- New Tiles view (the default) with just the icon and the name, as many across as fit. The View chip switches between Tiles and List and is remembered. Installed, loaded and updatable projects get a small mark on their icon.

**Fits GUI scale 4 and small windows** (0.12.2)
- Client screens, the title screen and the crash helper keep their layout on small windows and are drawn a little smaller instead of overlapping or running off the edge.
- HUD widgets shrink a little on small GUIs so the default layout no longer piles up (a switch on the HUD editor feature).
- Command Centre tiles scroll, long setting names wrap, and container panels shrink to fit beside the container.
